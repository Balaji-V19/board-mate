## Frequently asked questions

### How do I sign in?

Open BoardMate and tap **Continue with Apple** (on iPhone) or **Continue with Google**. We use your sign-in only to remember your saved games and progress across devices. We never see your Apple or Google password.

### Sign-in keeps failing

Try this in order:

1. Check that your device has internet access.
2. Quit the app fully and reopen it.
3. Restart your device.
4. On Android, make sure Google Play services is up to date.

If sign-in still fails, email us with the exact error message you see.

### I can't find one of my favourite games

BoardMate is growing the catalogue over time. To request a missing game, open **Settings → Request a game** and submit the title. We read every request and prioritise games with the most demand.

### A rule looks wrong or is missing

The guides are written by us and may simplify edge cases. If you spot something that seems incorrect, email us with the game name and the rule in question. We'll investigate and correct the content if needed.

### How do I delete my account and all my data?

Email vbalaji165@gmail.com from the email address on your Apple ID or Google account and ask to delete your BoardMate account. We process deletion requests within 7 business days and remove all the data covered by our [Privacy Policy](legal/PRIVACY.md).

You can also stop using BoardMate at any time by signing out (**Settings → Sign out**) — that simply ends the current session without deleting your data.

### Why does BoardMate need to sign me in?

Sign-in lets us:

- Save the games you bookmark and continue learning where you left off — even after reinstalling the app
- Sync your progress between devices (e.g. iPhone and iPad)
- Personalise the Home screen with your "Continue learning" card

If you'd rather not sign in, BoardMate is not the right app for you today. Anonymous / guest mode is on our roadmap.

### Is BoardMate affiliated with the publishers of these games?

No. BoardMate is an independent learning resource. Game names and rule concepts belong to their respective publishers. We write our own simplified guides from scratch; we do not redistribute publisher rulebooks or artwork.

### The reference photo on a game looks wrong

The cover and step photos are sourced from Wikimedia Commons and Unsplash and are credited under **Settings → Image credits**. If a photo is misleading or seems to misrepresent a game, email us and we'll swap it.

### Is the app free?

Yes, BoardMate is free to use today. We have not committed to keeping every feature free forever — if we ever introduce paid features, we will be upfront about it in advance.

### How is my data protected?

We use Firebase Authentication and Firebase Cloud Firestore (both operated by Google). Database security rules ensure that only you can read or write data linked to your account. We do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or any third-party trackers. Full details are in the [Privacy Policy](legal/PRIVACY.md).

## Reporting a bug

If something crashes, freezes, or visibly looks broken, please email us and include:

- A description of what you were doing when it happened
- A screenshot or screen recording if you can capture one
- The version + device info listed under **Contact** above

The more detail you can include, the faster we can reproduce and fix it.
