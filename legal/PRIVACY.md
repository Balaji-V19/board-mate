# BoardMate — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 18 May 2026**

This policy explains what information the BoardMate mobile app ("BoardMate", "the app", "we", "us") collects when you use it, how that information is used, and the choices you have. It is written in plain language; if anything is unclear, contact us at the address at the bottom of this page.

## Who we are

BoardMate is operated by Balaji Venkatachalam ("the developer") as an independent learning companion for physical board games. The app does not include digital gameplay; it provides setup walkthroughs, rule guides, turn flow, and quick references for tabletop games you already own.

## Information we collect

We collect the minimum information needed to give you a working account and to remember what you have saved or learned. Everything we collect comes either from you signing in or from your direct activity in the app.

**From your sign-in provider (Apple ID or Google account):**

- Your email address
- Your display name
- A URL to your profile photo (only if your provider supplies one)
- An anonymous user identifier ("UID") assigned by Firebase Authentication

**From your activity in the app:**

- The games you save (game identifiers only — no rating, comment, or annotation)
- Your learning progress — which sections of which games you have viewed or marked complete, and when you last viewed them
- Game requests you submit through the "Request a game" form (the game name, category, and any notes you choose to add)
- Bug reports or feedback you submit through the app

We do **not** collect:

- Location data
- Contacts, photos from your camera roll, or microphone input
- Browsing history or activity in other apps
- Advertising identifiers
- Sensitive personal data such as race, religion, health, or financial information

We do **not** use any third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks. There are no trackers in the app.

## How we use the information

We use the information described above only to operate BoardMate:

- Authenticate you when you open the app and keep you signed in
- Show your saved games, learning progress, and the "Continue learning" card on Home
- Send the rules and guide content you request from our servers to your device
- Triage the game requests and feedback you submit so we can decide which games to add next or fix incorrect content

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to profile you across other apps or services.

## Who processes the data on our behalf

BoardMate uses **Google Firebase** (operated by Google LLC) as the only third-party service. Firebase provides:

- Authentication (sign-in)
- Cloud Firestore (the database where your account information and saved games live)

Firebase's privacy practices are described at <https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy>. By using BoardMate, your data is processed under Firebase's terms in addition to this policy.

Game cover and reference photos shown inside the app are hosted on Wikimedia Commons and Unsplash and are loaded over HTTPS from your device. Those services may log your device's IP address when an image loads; we do not control or share that data.

## How long we keep the data

- Account data (email, name, photo URL, UID) is kept while your BoardMate account exists.
- Saved games and learning progress are kept while your account exists.
- Game requests and feedback are kept indefinitely for product improvement; you can ask us to delete a specific submission.

If you delete your account, we delete your account profile and your saved games / progress within 30 days. Some backup snapshots may retain the data for up to 90 days before being overwritten.

## Your rights and choices

You can:

- **Access** the data we have about you — email the contact below and we will send it.
- **Correct** your display name by signing in again with an updated Apple ID or Google profile.
- **Delete** your account and all the data linked to it — email the contact below. We process deletion requests within 7 business days.
- **Sign out** at any time from **Settings → Sign out**. Signing out does not delete your data.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR (European Union), the UK Data Protection Act, or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act. To exercise those rights, contact us at the address below.

## Security

Sign-in is handled by Apple, Google, and Firebase Authentication using industry-standard protocols. Your data in Firestore is protected by security rules that allow only you to read or write data under your account. We never see or store your Apple or Google password.

No transmission over the internet is 100% secure; if you believe your account has been compromised, please contact us so we can help.

## Children

BoardMate is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will delete the information.

## International transfers

Firebase services may store and process data in countries outside your country of residence. By using BoardMate, you consent to this processing.

## Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top and, when appropriate, notify you the next time you open the app. Continued use of BoardMate after a change indicates acceptance of the revised policy.
